What Is DMAIC?

DMAIC is a five-phase problem-solving method used to improve processes, reduce errors, and enhance performance. Each step builds on the one before it:

  • Define: What is the problem?
  • Measure: How bad is the problem?
  • Analyze: Why is it happening?
  • Improve: How do we fix it?
  • Control: How do we keep the improvement?
